Privacy Policy – Albion Tracker Mobile

Last updated: February 25, 2026

Albion Tracker Mobile ("the App") is a companion app for the game Albion Online that displays real-time stats such as damage, fame, silver gained, gathering and dungeon data, and allows party stat sharing.

1. Information We Collect

  • In-game character name: You voluntarily enter your Albion Online character name. This name is uploaded to Firebase Realtime Database solely to enable real-time party stat sharing with other players in your group.
  • Game session stats: Damage dealt, fame gained, silver gained, and equipment data are processed in real time and temporarily stored in Firebase during an active party session.
  • Device identifiers: Google AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • App usage data: AdMob may collect information about ad interactions (views, clicks) and app performance data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Character name and session stats are used exclusively to provide the party sync feature of the App.
  • Device and usage data collected by AdMob is used to display advertisements within the App.
  • We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google Firebase Realtime Database – used to sync party stats in real time. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob – used to display ads. Google Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention

Party session data (character name and stats) stored in Firebase is removed automatically when a party session ends. No long-term personal data is stored by the App developer.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Data Security

All data transmitted by the App is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

7. Your Rights

You may stop using the App at any time. Since no account is created, there is no persistent personal data stored by the developer beyond the current session. For data held by Google (Firebase/AdMob), please refer to Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
